TWO TABLETS
DUE
GENERAL
DUE holds anything with a date, grouped into Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This Week and Later. GENERAL holds everything else. Sort or shuffle it however you like. Give a task a date (TODAY, TMRW or 📅) when you add it and it goes to DUE. Otherwise it goes to GENERAL.
HOW IT SAVES
GOOGLE SIGN-IN
CLOUD SYNC
WORKS OFFLINE
Tasks are saved to your Google account and sync to every device you sign in on. Offline? Keep adding. Changes sync when you reconnect.
PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE
iPhone: open the site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: open the site in Chrome → ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.
DASHBOARD MODE
Open MENU → DASHBOARD on a spare screen. It shows big, live-updating lists, a clock, and keeps the screen awake. Tap FULLSCREEN for a clean display.
BRINGING OLD TASKS OVER
On the old Neocities site, press EXPORT on each device to download a .json file. Here, open MENU → IMPORT JSON and choose that file. Old time-sensitive tasks land in DUE under "Needs a date." Duplicates are skipped, so importing twice is safe.
